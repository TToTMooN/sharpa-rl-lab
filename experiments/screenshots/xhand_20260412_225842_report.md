*   **Cylinder Floating with No Support:** **[CRITICAL]** The cylinder is not in a stable initial grasp. It appears to be floating just above the fingertips without definite contact. ## 5. Verdict

**Justification:** The core premise of in-hand rotation training starting from a stable grasp (≥3 contacts) is violated. The cylinder is floating above the hand with zero contact points in the initial pose. The policy would first need to perform a grasp or catch before any rotation could begin. This initial setup is fundamentally unstable and inappropriate for the specified training objective.
